Closed-loop learning

How the adaptive lifecycle works

A continuous loop that diagnoses proficiency, closes knowledge gaps and proves the result.

01

Curriculum synthesis

Any domain is mapped into a knowledge tree: subject → domains → topics → concepts, with prerequisites.

02

Adaptive examination

Each next question is chosen to match the candidate's current ability estimate — no wasted items.

03

Bayesian diagnostics

Mastery is tracked per concept with confidence weights, so a high total can't hide a weak spot.

04

Targeted retesting

Socratic micro-lessons for weak concepts, then a focused retest with brand-new, unseen items.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does AI grade my answers?

No. Generative AI is used to create curricula, questions and explanations. Every response is scored by a deterministic Bayesian Item Response Theory (3PL) engine, so the same answers always produce the same result.

What is Item Response Theory, in plain words?

IRT scores a learner by taking into account how hard each question is, how well it separates strong from weak learners, and how likely a correct guess is. The result is an ability estimate (θ) with a stated margin of error — far more precise than a raw percentage.

Can I use Sensighty for my own subject?

Yes. Sensighty treats subjects as data. Describe or upload a subject and it builds the domain → topic → concept structure and a question bank, which educators can review and approve before use.

How are certificates verified?

Each certificate is tied to its assessment record by a SHA-256 hash and has a public verification URL and QR code. Anyone — an employer, a university — can confirm it's genuine and unaltered.

Will learners see the same questions on a retest?

No. Retests draw new, unseen items focused on the concepts the learner struggled with, so they measure understanding rather than memory.
